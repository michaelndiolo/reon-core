# Questions for REON (Nalia asks in person)

Rules for asking: in person or private DM only, never in a public channel. Ask the question as written. Do not say what we built, which designs we compare, or what numbers we got. If a mentor asks "why do you want to know", the answer is "so our comparison matches how your product really works".

Best windows (Sat 7 Nov / Sun 8 Nov, from the Luma schedule): Sat lunch 1:00-2:30 PM, Sat dinner 6-8 PM, Sun breakfast 8:00-9:30 AM (before the 9:30 semi-finals), Sun sponsor info sessions 12:30-1:30 PM. Coffee-chat blocks on Sat are Emera/COMSOL only. The page lists no REON mentor hours or deadline: also ask the organizers (check-in desk Lobby 10, Discord organizers, mitec-hack@mit.edu).

## A. Must-ask (each one changes a number in our answer)

1. Does your hardware let you bypass any single cell, or only groups of cells? Is it one switch per cell?
2. Roughly what does the switching hardware add per cell, or per kWh, at the volumes you quote? A range is fine.
3. When a cell is in the path and when it is bypassed, what is the extra resistance or efficiency loss? Is there any standby power?
4. For the baseline, should we assume a plain fixed series string with no balancing at all, or a string with a passive or active balancer? If a balancer, which current and cost should we assume?
5. Retirement: do you want 80 % (EV) and 70 % (stationary) judged on the weakest cell, or on usable pack capacity? Who decides, and is there a minimum usable energy?
6. Which of the three applications (retired EV packs, grid storage, data-center backup) matters most to you, and what depth of discharge and charge rate does it typically run?

## B. Data and evidence

7. Is there any cell-level data (capacity per cell over time, or a distribution of cell capacities in retired packs) you can share or point us to?
8. In the packs you see, are weak cells spread evenly, or are there a few clear outliers? Roughly what share?
9. How do you handle a cell that has failed or gone open-circuit: detection, bypass, repair? Do you have a rough fault rate per cell per year?

## C. Submission and judging

10. What is the exact submission format (dashboard link, slides, both, repository) and the deadline?
11. How are the judging criteria weighted: technical depth, economic value, environmental value, clarity?
12. Is a live demo needed, and how long is the pitch?
13. Are assumptions taken from public sources acceptable, if they are stated clearly?
14. Where and when will REON mentors be on site on Saturday and Sunday?

## D. Environmental value (only if time allows)

15. Do you prefer the carbon claim counted as avoided new-cell manufacturing, or as lifetime energy throughput? Which emission factor per kWh of manufacturing do you use?

## What we are NOT telling them
No design names, no win rates, no break-even numbers, no mention that we test against balancers beyond the baseline question.
