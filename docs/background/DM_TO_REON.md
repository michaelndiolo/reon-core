# DM to REON (Michael sends it himself; private, never in a public channel)

Hi, I'm Michael from the team working on the "Beyond the Weakest Cell" challenge (with Aaryan and Nalia). To make our comparison match how your product really works, could you answer whichever of these you can? Rough ranges are fine, and "can't share" is a fine answer. Happy to take any of them in person instead.

HARDWARE
1. Can your module bypass each individual cell, or only groups of cells? If groups, how many cells per group? Is bypass fully in or out, or can a cell be partly bypassed?
2. Roughly what does the bypass and sensing hardware add per cell, or per kWh of pack? A range is fine, and a price at about 1,000 cells and at about 100,000 cells would be ideal. Does that include the controller and sensing, or only the switch?
3. What extra resistance or efficiency loss does a cell see when it is in the current path, and what when it is bypassed? Is there standby power per cell or per module?
4. Besides bypass, does your hardware move charge between cells? If so, at roughly what current and efficiency?
5. In practice, what stops a cell being bypassed: voltage window of the inverter or converter, a minimum number of cells in a string, or a safety limit?

BASELINE AND RULES
6. The brief says the baseline is a plain fixed series string without effective redistribution. Should we assume no balancing of any kind, or a passive balancer, or an active one? If balanced, what current and cost per kWh would you consider fair?
7. May cells be tested and sorted before assembly in the baseline pack?
8. For the 80 % (EV) and 70 % (stationary) retirement thresholds: is that measured on the weakest cell, on the usable capacity of the string, or on the whole pack? Who decides, and is there a minimum usable energy?
9. For a retired EV pack reused as stationary storage, which threshold applies, and from which starting capacity (original new cell or the capacity at the start of second life)?

APPLICATIONS
10. Of retired EV packs, grid storage and data-center backup, which do you most want a worked answer for? For that one, what depth of discharge, charge rate and cycles per day should we assume, over how many years?
11. For data-center backup, do the packs cycle regularly, or sit at full charge and get replaced on age?
12. What value should we put on a kWh of delivered or deferred energy in each application, or which price source do you prefer?

DATA AND FAULTS
13. Is there any cell-level data you can share or point us to: capacity of every cell at several ages, or the distribution of cell capacities in a retired pack? Even one anonymised pack would help a lot.
14. In the packs you have seen, are weak cells spread evenly, or are there a few clear outliers? About what share of cells is far below the rest?
15. How does your system detect and bypass a failed or open-circuit cell? Roughly what share of cells fail per year, and are the failures usually repairable (for example a loose contact) or permanent?

ENVIRONMENT
16. For the carbon claim, do you prefer avoided new-cell manufacturing, or lifetime energy throughput? Which manufacturing emission factor per kWh do you use, and do you count the grid benefit on average or marginal emissions?

SUBMISSION
17. What is the exact submission format (dashboard link, slides, both, repository) and the deadline?
18. How are the criteria weighted: technical depth, economic value, environmental value, clarity of the dashboard? Do judges run the dashboard themselves?
19. Is a live demo expected, and how long is the pitch?
20. Is it fine to base assumptions on public data if we state them clearly?
21. Where and when will REON mentors be on site on Saturday and Sunday?

If it is easier, tell me one time to find you and I'll bring this list. Thank you!
