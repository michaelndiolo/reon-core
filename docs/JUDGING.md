# Judging criteria and where our evidence sits (MIT Energy & Climate Hack 2026)

Semifinals: written submission only. Weights: Scope 15, Interdisciplinary 15, Feasibility 25, Creativity 20, Impact 25. Finals add a live pitch (15) and shift Feasibility to 20, Creativity to 15, Impact to 20.

| Criterion | A 5 looks like | What we have | Gap to close |
|---|---|---|---|
| Scope | Sharp problem, right size, answers what the sponsor asked | REON asked for a dashboard of what cell-level control is worth against a plain series string in one real application. We pick C&I stationary sodium, with a decision layer on top of REON's hardware | Keep one hero case in the write-up; EV and data-center are toggles only |
| Interdisciplinary | Several lenses that shape each other | Cell physics and ageing, money (net value, break-even, policy choice), reliability (faults, crews, storms), user (which cell hurts, what to do), hardware interface draft | Show the chain on one page: spread, then stranded kWh, then dollars, then the action |
| Feasibility | Evidence, working prototype, honest limits | 230 passing tests, real cell data (12 cars), sourced inputs tagged REAL / CITED / ASSUMED, hostile stress tests, fuzzed inputs, dashboard that says HOLD or "does not pay here", and a documented list of what is unknown | Sodium fade and spread are unmeasured; fault rate, visit cost and inverter minimum voltage are assumed. Say so on page 6 |
| Creativity | An angle the sponsor likely had not considered | Automatic bypass for sites no crew can reach, a per-site policy chooser, the cost of waiting for a crew, and reporting where our method loses | Make the "automatic backup generator for a battery string" line the headline |
| Impact | Large, quantified, credible path to scale | Net value per kWh against plain, rival and threshold policies, with ranges; backup hours in storms; fewer crew visits | Quantify at fleet scale once Tim gives a price and a fault rate; do not claim capacity gains at 20,000-cycle fade |
| Pitch (finals) | Compelling story, strong demo, on time | Dashboard tabs 1 to 7 | Demo script; lead with the story of the string that fixes itself |
